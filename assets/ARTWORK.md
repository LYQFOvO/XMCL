Create a cinematic wide 16:9 landscape of a Minecraft-style voxel world: deep evergreen spruce forest, blocky moss-covered mountains on the right, quiet turquoise lake curving from center to right foreground, a tiny warm-lit wooden cabin nestled on the right shore, distant layered mountains and soft mist, luminous cloudy pale sage sky, subtle sunbeams from upper right. Every terrain and tree shape is distinctly voxel/block based, with sophisticated shader-like atmospheric lighting and beautiful water reflections. Calm immersive adventure mood, deep forest greens, desaturated teal, pale warm light. Wide composition: left third darker trees and mist providing quiet negative space for overlaid UI text, most scenic detail toward right two thirds; foreground dark. High-end game environment render, rich detail without visual clutter. NO text, NO interface, NO logos, NO watermark, NO people. Landscape 1792x1024 or similar wide resolution.
